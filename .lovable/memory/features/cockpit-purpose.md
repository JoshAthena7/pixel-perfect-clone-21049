---
name: Cockpit purpose
description: Cockpit is not a drafting surface — drafts live in the client's environment; Cockpit provides intel, health checks, scoring, and collaboration per question
type: feature
---
Drafts are NOT written inside Athena/Atlas. Writers compose answers in the client's own environment (Word, Google Docs, their proposal tool, etc.).

The Cockpit (per-question workspace at `/missions/$missionId/sections/$questionId` and the assigned-question list at `/cockpit`) is the intelligence + collaboration layer around each question. It provides:
- Mission intel relevant to the question (graph-tagged signals)
- Health checks (red/yellow/green, compliance notes, gaps)
- Scoring (Score-Me, confidence)
- Collaboration (Phone a Friend, Get Help, SME routing, Broadcasts)

Never describe Cockpit as a draft editor, document editor, or writing surface in copy, tooltips, onboarding, or UI labels. CTAs like "Open in Cockpit" / "Enter Cockpit" mean "open the intel + health workspace for this question," not "start writing."
